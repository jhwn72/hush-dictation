Settings

Shortcuts

DictateHold to talk. Double-tap for hands-free (tap again to stop), Esc cancels.
CommandHold and speak an instruction. With text selected it rewrites it ("make this shorter"); with nothing selected it writes what you ask. If it includes the dictate keys, you can also start dictating and add the extra key.
Voice commandsEnd a dictation with "press enter" or "send it" to submit, or "scratch that" to drop the last sentence. Say "scratch that" on its own to remove the previous dictation. "New line" and "new paragraph" add breaks.
ModeHold = push-to-talk. Toggle = press once to start, again to stop.
TransformsModifier keys + slot number, on selected text.
